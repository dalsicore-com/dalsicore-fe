Designing assistive color tools requires more than remapping algorithms. The interface layer determines whether users can act confidently on what they see.

## Observations

- Labels should be concise and contextual, not purely technical.
- Contrast and shape cues are still critical even after color transformation.
- Real-time overlays must avoid visual clutter.

## Working hypothesis

The most useful solution will likely combine lightweight color correction with explainable guidance at key decision points.

## Next iteration

The next round will test micro-interactions for instant color clarification in dynamic scenes.
