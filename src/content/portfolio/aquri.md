## Why this product

Aquri starts from a simple insight: most hydration apps fail because they feel like manual logging tools. This product focuses on reducing friction, providing adaptive reminders, and creating clear progress loops.

## Product direction

- Turn daily logs into meaningful hydration insights users can actually use.
- Let the app adapt to the user, not the other way around.
- Focus on consistency through simple feedback like streaks and daily progress.
